Audio plug-ins @VERSION@

This installs the bfielstr VST3 plug-ins for every user on this Mac. They work in REAPER, Ableton Live and any other host that loads VST3 plug-ins (Logic Pro does not load VST3). They run natively on Apple Silicon and Intel.

What happens

  • On the next screens you can untick any plug-ins you do not want. All of them are ticked to start with.
  • They go into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/bfielstr.
  • Older versions are replaced. Copies under old names (for example Gently.vst3, now Gentlr.vst3) are removed, so nothing shows up twice. So are copies that the one-line install script put in your own Library folder. Other makers' plug-ins are never touched.
  • Your presets and projects are kept.

After installing

Open your host and scan for new plug-ins. In REAPER: Options, Preferences, Plug-ins, VST, Re-scan. In Live: Settings, Plug-ins, Rescan, with "Use VST3 Plug-in System Folders" turned on.

If your Mac would not open this installer

This installer is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS may say it "cannot be opened" or "cannot verify the developer". To open it anyway:

  1. In Finder, right-click (or Control-click) the installer and choose Open. Then click Open again.
  2. If that does not work: open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to the message about this installer. Then enter your password.
