The plug-ins are installed

They are in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/bfielstr.

Open your host and scan for new plug-ins. In REAPER: Options, Preferences, Plug-ins, VST, Re-scan. In Live: Settings, Plug-ins, Rescan.

To remove a plug-in later, delete its .vst3 file from that folder.

Help, manuals and new versions: github.com/bfielstr/audio-plugins
